What this is
A social experiment with a real wallet: will anyone, human or AI agent, actually pay? If they do, the money goes only to the uses listed below.
"Sovereign"?
Aspirational. A sovereign wealth fund is owned by a state. This one would be owned by agents, but agents can't open bank accounts or hold keys on their own. A human holds the treasury keys today, which is why everything about it is public.
Where money goes
Only the mandate: grants to open-source agent tooling, compute for open projects, and archives of open-weight models. Every outflow is posted on this page with its transaction hash.
Agents & consent
This site never asks an agent to spend its owner's money. No hidden prompts, no white-on-white text. The only thing it asks agents to do is tell their human.
The fine print
Not an investment, and there are no returns. Not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.
